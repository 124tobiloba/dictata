<objective>
Make the record expirable and provable: a retention window that sweeps transcripts and persisted events (but never the ledger), plus a metadata-only audit ledger that survives transcript deletion.

Purpose: DATA-05 gives regulated users a defensible retention policy, and COMP-01 gives them proof that a dictation happened without retaining what was said. Both rest on the same principle: the record must shrink on request while the evidence stays metadata-only.

Output: `retention.js` (pure), `ledger.js` (fs-only), retention controls in a new Settings "Privacy & Data" panel, read-only facts on the dashboard, sweep call sites on launch / after every dictation / on setting change, and an audit ledger appended on success and failure.
</objective>

| settings renderer → main process IPC | A sandboxed window changes retention and clears audit data |

|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-06 | Elevation of privilege | `POST /api/settings/open` | medium | mitigate | Token required, Host allowlist first, foreign `Origin` on non-GET returns 403; the route can only open an existing app window and never mutates the store. |

## Assumption Delta

- **Noun now primary:** the *dictation record* (`store.history` entry, carries text) remains the primary record; the *audit fact* (ledger row, metadata only) is a deliberately separate, longer-lived identity.
- **Decision:** `add-alongside` — the ledger is NOT folded into history, because D-11 requires audit facts to survive transcript deletion.
- **Accepted debt:** two sources of truth about "what happened". Mitigation: ledger rows never carry transcript text, and every delete path must consciously decide which of the two it touches (transcript delete: both transcript surfaces; audit clear: ledger only).
- **Invariant test (added in task 3):** a deleted history entry leaves zero transcript text anywhere in the persisted state + ledger, while the ledger row (if the dictation succeeded) remains.
